Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 28, 2025

1. Introduction

DessoyRacing ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Publisher application ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App to manage social media content for DessoyRacing across Facebook and Instagram.

2. Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following types of information:

  • Social Media Account Information: When you connect your Facebook or Instagram account, we access basic profile information necessary for posting content.
  • Content Data: Posts, images, captions, and hashtags you create within the App.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the App, stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Post content to your connected Facebook and Instagram accounts
  • Tag sponsors and apply hashtags as specified by you
  • Store your preferences and templates locally on your device
  • Provide the core functionality of the social media management tool

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device in JSON format. We do not transmit your personal information to external servers except when directly posting to Facebook and Instagram through their official APIs. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): To post content to your social media accounts. Please review Meta's Privacy Policy at https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Information is only shared with Meta's platforms as necessary to post your content.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored locally in the App
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Disconnect your social media accounts
  • Request information about how your data is processed

8. Data Retention

Data is stored locally on your device and retained until you choose to delete it. You can delete all data by removing the App or clearing its local storage files.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
