Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: December 28, 2025

1. Overview

This Data Deletion Policy explains how users of the DessoyRacing Publisher application can request deletion of their data. We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing you with control over your personal information.

2. What Data We Store

The DessoyRacing Publisher application stores the following data:

  • Facebook and Instagram account connection tokens
  • Social media post templates and drafts
  • Sponsor information and tagging preferences
  • Hashtag lists and posting preferences
  • Application settings and configurations
Important: All data is stored locally on your device. No data is stored on external servers operated by DessoyRacing.

3. How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete Data Locally (Immediate)

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it immediately by:

  1. Uninstalling the DessoyRacing Publisher application from your device
  2. Or, within the App, navigating to Settings and selecting "Clear All Data"
  3. Or, manually deleting the application's data folder on your device

This will permanently remove all locally stored information.

Option 2: Request Data Deletion Assistance

If you need assistance with data deletion, you can contact us:

  1. Send an email to rdessoy@googlemail.com
  2. Include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line
  3. Provide your Facebook/Instagram username (if applicable)
  4. Describe what data you would like deleted

We will respond to your request within 30 days and provide confirmation once completed.

4. What Happens When You Delete Your Data

When you delete your data from the DessoyRacing Publisher application:

  • All locally stored templates, drafts, and preferences will be permanently removed
  • Your Facebook and Instagram account connections will be disconnected
  • Application settings will be reset to defaults
  • Any saved sponsor information and hashtag lists will be deleted
Note: Deleting data from the Publisher app does not delete posts that have already been published to Facebook or Instagram. You must delete those directly from the respective social media platforms.

5. Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Data

To delete data held by Meta about your use of this application:

  • Visit your Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • Find "DessoyRacing Publisher" in the list
  • Click "Remove" to revoke access and delete associated data

For more information, see Meta's data deletion instructions at: https://www.facebook.com/help/delete_account

6. Data Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Data deleted locally when you uninstall the app or clear data
  • Email Requests: Responded to within 30 days
  • Meta Platform Data: Follows Meta's deletion timeline (typically immediate upon revocation)

7. Contact Us

For questions about data deletion or to submit a deletion request:

Email: rdessoy@googlemail.com

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - DessoyRacing Publisher

Website: dessoyracing.com

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Data Deletion Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
