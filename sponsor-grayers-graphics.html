About Grayers Graphics

Grayers Graphics is a highly regarded company specializing in vehicle wraps, sign writing, and graphic design, with over 15 years of experience in the sign making trade. Based in Grays, Essex, they are known for their exceptional quality of work and dedication to creating visually striking and impactful designs that help businesses stand out.

The company specializes in commercial vehicle wrapping, fleet livery design, supply, and installation. From vehicle graphics and wraps to rally car livery, signage, and shop front graphics, Grayers Graphics provides comprehensive branding solutions that enhance visibility and professional image.

Their impressive portfolio includes wrapping the coach for Brazil's national football team with only eight hours' notice - a testament to their capability to deliver outstanding results under tight deadlines. Customers consistently highlight their exceptional quality in both vehicle wraps and signage, with dedicated customer service that supports clients throughout every project.
