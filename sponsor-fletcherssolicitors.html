About Fletchers Solicitors

Fletchers Solicitors is one of the UK's most trusted and respected personal injury and medical negligence law firms. With offices across the country — including Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool, and Newcastle — they provide expert legal support to individuals who have suffered serious injuries, helping them to Recover. Rebuild. Thrive.

Their specialist teams handle everything from road traffic accidents and serious injury claims — including spinal injuries, brain injuries, and amputations — to medical negligence, birth injury, and international accident cases. Fletchers operates exclusively on a No Win, No Fee basis, meaning access to expert legal representation is available to everyone who needs it, regardless of financial circumstance.

Recognised by both Legal 500 and Chambers as a top-tier firm, and Lexcel accredited, Fletchers has built its reputation on delivering life-changing results for clients across the UK. Their dedicated rehabilitation and wellbeing team ensures that clients receive comprehensive support throughout and beyond the legal process.
