About ARC-ON

ARC-ON is a UK-based specialist manufacturer of award-winning motorcycle race gloves, with over 16 years of innovation and dedication to rider safety. Founded on the principles of providing the highest levels of protection without compromising on comfort or performance, ARC-ON has become a trusted name among professional racers worldwide.

Their flagship 034 RS gloves represent the pinnacle of glove technology, featuring the revolutionary Kozane Invicta textile armour lining. This cutting-edge material provides exceptional abrasion and puncture resistance while maintaining the flexibility and comfort that racers demand during intense competition.

ARC-ON gloves are extensively tested and trusted by BSB (British Superbike Championship) and World Endurance Championship riders, proving their performance at the highest levels of motorcycle racing. This professional-grade protection is exactly what Harrison needs as he competes on the world stage.
