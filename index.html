Hello, I am Harrison Dessoy, a 20 year old motorcycle racer from Thurrock, Essex. In 2026, I am competing in the brand new 2026 World Sportbike Championship, and super excited to be competing against some fast and experienced competition.

With over 8 years of racing experience, starting in the Cool Fab Racing British Minibikes Championship, I embark on the world stage with my PHR Performance racing team, on the Triumph Daytona 660. With a love for physical training, mental prep work and dedication, I massively look forward to such a prestigious opportunity in 2026.

I am so excited to be showcasing my skills on the world stage in 2026. Riding the Triumph Daytona 660 with the PHR Performance team, I am confident to be pushing against some of the worlds best riders!
