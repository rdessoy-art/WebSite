About Rock Cameras

Rock Cameras is a London-based specialist in camera rentals and photographic digital equipment. They offer an extensive range of rental cameras and digital equipment, available for dry hire or wet hire with one of their experienced Digital Operators — making professional-grade imaging accessible to productions of all sizes.

As an authorised stockist of premium brands including Inovativ, Area51 Tether cables, Mule Carts, Fotofortress, Rock Tether Cables, Calipso, and Swift Bklyn, Rock Cameras brings together the very best in professional imaging technology under one roof. Their team combines deep technical expertise with a hands-on approach, ensuring every client gets the right kit for the job.

With a loyalty rewards programme and a commitment to beating any UK price on brands they stock, Rock Cameras is the go-to partner for photographers and filmmakers who demand the highest standards from their equipment.
