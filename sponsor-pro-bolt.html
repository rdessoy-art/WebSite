About Pro Bolt

Pro Bolt is a leading UK manufacturer and supplier of premium titanium and stainless steel fasteners for motorcycles. With years of experience in the motorsport industry, Pro Bolt has built a reputation for producing high-quality, lightweight, and durable components that meet the demanding requirements of professional racing.

Specialising in titanium fasteners, Pro Bolt offers significant weight savings without compromising strength or reliability. Their extensive range includes bolts, nuts, washers, and specialty fasteners designed specifically for motorcycles, making them the go-to choice for racers looking to optimise every aspect of their machine's performance.

Pro Bolt's products are engineered to exacting standards, offering superior corrosion resistance and aesthetic appeal. Their titanium range is particularly popular in the racing community, where every gram counts and durability under extreme conditions is essential.
